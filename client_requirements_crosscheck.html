The 6 Modular n8n Workflows (Master Router Pattern)

We divide the system into 6 isolated workflows governed by a single master router to prevent unnecessary executions and eliminate wasted API credits:

WORKFLOW 01 · MASTER ROUTER

Email Ingest & Triage

The only workflow connected to your mailbox. When an email arrives, it checks customer history, classifies the email in 300ms, and routes it to the single appropriate sub-workflow.

WORKFLOW 02 · SUB-WORKFLOW

Technical Support & Manuals

Wakes up only for technical questions. Checks uploaded manuals, drafts troubleshooting instructions, or requests battery photos if needed.

WORKFLOW 03 · SUB-WORKFLOW

Field Calculator & Quotes

Wakes up only for quotation requests. Calculates travel, technician hours ($150/hr), and hardware costs using your exact Excel formulas, then prepares your Slack card.

WORKFLOW 04 · SUB-WORKFLOW

Tender & RFP Parser

Wakes up only when tender documents or RFP attachments arrive. Extracts submission deadlines and technical specs, posting an urgent alert in Slack.

WORKFLOW 05 · WEBHOOK TRIGGER

Slack Approval Handler

No email trigger. Wakes up only when you tap [Approve] or [Edit] in Slack. Instantly sends the final email and updates your CRM.

WORKFLOW 06 · CRON TIMER

Automated Follow-ups

No email trigger. Runs once a day at 9:00 AM. Checks for unreplied quotes (3/7 days), aging batteries (3+ years), and approaching tender dates.

⚡ ZERO WASTED API CREDITS (ROUTER PATTERN)

When an email arrives, only Workflow 1 triggers. It uses a fast classifier (costing ~$0.0001) and passes data to only one sub-workflow. All other workflows stay asleep with zero credit usage.
